What is collected
{{ minimum_samples }} or more still photos of the employee's face, taken with this computer's camera.
What is created from the photos
From each accepted photo, the app calculates a face embedding: a list of 128 numbers that describes the face so that two photos can be compared. Face embeddings are biometric data and are protected in the same way as the photos.
Why
To recognise the employee when marking attendance in this portfolio application.
Where it is kept
Photos in a private folder on this computer, outside the website's public files, under random file names; face embeddings in the app's private local database. Neither photos nor embeddings are shown on any page, written to logs or sent to another service.
Your choice
Consent can be withdrawn at any time. Withdrawing deletes every stored face photo and every face embedding made from them. Attendance history is kept.
